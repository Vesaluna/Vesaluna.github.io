This is a reading guide, not a reproduction of the full text. Peng Minghui writes about anxiety over education, work, and life's decisions: one choice need not be treated as an isolated event capable of determining an entire life. Long-term accumulation matters more than a momentary gain or loss. I saved the piece as a prompt to revisit my own anxiety about the pace of study.

A [verifiable online copy](https://gupa.gzhu.edu.cn/info/1081/5080.htm) appears on Guangzhou University's website, which attributes it to a bulletin of National Tsing Hua University's Department of Electrical Engineering. I could not verify a stable page for the original bulletin. The date here records when I created my local note, not when the essay first appeared.
