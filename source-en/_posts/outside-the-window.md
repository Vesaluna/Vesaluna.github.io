Lin Huiyin's essay makes the window a persistent distance between the observer and the lives outside. The view changes—from a study to a carriage to a journey—but the separation remains. Reading it, I ask whether I am taking part in a world or merely looking at it.

Author: Lin Huiyin. First published in the literary supplement of *Ta Kung Pao* on 5 September 1934; see this [online transcription](https://millionbook.net/xd/l/linhuiyin/000/001.htm). The date shown here is when I created my local note. For now this page offers a guide and a link to the text.
