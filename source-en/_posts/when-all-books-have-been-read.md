This is a reading guide, not a reproduction of the full essay. Jin Kemu begins with the seemingly impossible question of whether one can finish reading the old books. His concern is not simply their number, but their relationships: a small number of foundational works support many later discussions. It reminds me to ask whether I can see those connections, instead of merely adding titles to a list.

Author: Jin Kemu. The essay appeared in *Yan Zhuo Chun Ni* (People's Daily Press, 1987); an [online reprint at The Paper](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_8588860) provides a verifiable text. I found no original author-run website. The date here records when I created my local note.
