This is a reading guide, not a reproduction of the full page. Ravi Vakil wrote it for prospective doctoral students who might work with him, but it also discusses broader habits of research: finding questions, attending seminars, talking with colleagues, and moving beyond the goal of mastering every course towards problems with no known answer. It reminds me that learning mathematics is not only a solitary matter of finishing a book.

Author and original site: Ravi Vakil, on his [Stanford University personal page](https://math.stanford.edu/~vakil/potentialstudents.html). Its admissions details may be out of date; I link it here as reading about study and research. The date here records when I created my local note.
