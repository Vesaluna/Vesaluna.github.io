This is a reading guide, not a copy of the whole list. It gathers undergraduate textbooks and references across subjects such as analysis and algebra. It can help when choosing a first textbook by showing how many different treatments exist. Its assessments mix the original compiler's judgments with later editorial comments; I would use it as a starting point for looking up books, not as a definitive buying or study order.

A verifiable version is the [edited reprint on itdevwu Blog](https://blog.itdevwu.com/post/350/) (credited to itdevwu, 2020). That page says its source was a [PDF from Shanghai Jiao Tong University's School of Mathematical Sciences](http://math.sjtu.edu.cn/upload/teachers/3162/%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6%E7%B3%BB%E6%9C%AC%E7%A7%91%E4%B8%93%E4%B8%9A%E5%AD%A6%E4%B9%A0%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E4%B9%A6.pdf), but does not clearly identify the original individual author. I could not confirm that the PDF link still works; I distinguish the source from the later editor here. The date is when I created my local note.
